Here's a simple economic truth most people miss: when someone with a drug addiction finally sees the total bill, they may realize with a shiver that the cheapest part of the whole thing was the drug itself.
The stupidest illusion is saving up enough cash to buy the first fix and thinking, “I paid for it. Now the happiness is mine.”
No.
That first payment isn't the end of the transaction. It's the moment the real bill begins.
The instant that first dollar changes hands, the addiction cash register starts ringing.
